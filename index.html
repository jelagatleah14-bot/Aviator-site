🔎 Scam-awareness checklist

1. Check the domain

Look carefully at the spelling of the domain. Don't assume a familiar logo means you're visiting the legitimate company.

2. Beware of urgency

"Deposit now", "guaranteed win" and "account expires today" messages can be designed to prevent careful verification.

3. Never disclose PINs

A suspicious site asking for a mobile-money PIN, OTP or banking credential should immediately stop the transaction.

4. Don't trust screenshots

Balances, winning notifications, testimonials and transaction histories can all be fabricated with JavaScript.

5. Verify independently

Find the operator independently rather than relying on links supplied by an advert, WhatsApp message or social-media account.

6. A balance isn't proof

A number displayed on a webpage is not evidence that money actually exists in a regulated financial account.
